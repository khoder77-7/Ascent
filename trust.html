How we handle your documents

When you upload a transcript, essay, CV, or recommendation letter, here's the lifecycle:

  • Files are encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • They're used only to generate your matches and your application analysis — never sold, and never used to train third-party AI models.
  • You can delete any uploaded document, or your entire account, at any time from your dashboard settings. Deletion removes the underlying files, typically within 30 days, from our backups.
  • A small internal team can access documents only to fix a reported bug or respond to a support request you've raised — not to browse.
In short: your documents exist to help you get funded, not to build a dataset. If we ever change that, we'll ask first.

AI-generated suggestions

The scoring, match percentages, and essay feedback you see throughout Veya are generated by AI models, not human reviewers. That means two things worth being direct about:

  • AI feedback can be wrong, miss context, or misjudge tone — treat every score and suggestion as a second opinion, not a verdict.
  • You are always the author. Veya is built to diagnose and coach, not to write or rewrite your essays for you — anything AI-generated is a starting point for your own judgment, not a final answer.

We'd rather you double-check a suggestion and find it unnecessary than trust it blindly and regret it in a submitted application.

We don't guarantee outcomes

Veya improves the odds that you find scholarships you're genuinely eligible for and that your applications are stronger when you submit them. It cannot and does not guarantee:

  • That you will win any specific scholarship
  • That a "match %" reflects an actual selection committee's decision
  • That an application-strength score corresponds directly to admission or funding odds

Match scores and strength scores are estimates based on the eligibility criteria and rubric signals we can observe — they are directional, not promises.

Privacy policy (summary)

Last updated · August 2026 — plain-language summary, not legal advice

What we collect

Profile information you provide (academic, financial, and background details), documents you upload, and basic usage data (pages visited, features used) to keep the product working and improve it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data or documents to third parties.
  • We don't share your financial-situation details with scholarship providers without your explicit action (e.g., submitting an application through us).
  • We don't use your uploaded essays or documents to train AI models outside of generating your own results.

Your controls

  • Export or delete your profile and documents at any time from account settings.
  • Opt out of non-essential emails at any time.
  • Request a full account deletion, which removes your data from active systems and, within 30 days, from backups.

Third parties we rely on

We use infrastructure and AI providers (for hosting, database, and language-model scoring) under contracts that prohibit them from using your data for their own purposes. A full vendor list is available on request.
